## Intent

Context: Original fictional example. The written scene is the complete product specification for this example, not evidence of a shipped product.

## Composition contract
Archetype: object-detail

The entire base and dial remain visible. No display, cable, buttons, markings or extra parts. Soft studio light makes the ribs readable.
Elements:
- One warm-gray matte rounded rectangular timer base

- One small blue index notch on the dial top
